Two systems from this year

COMPASS

Market and demand intelligence for AARAV Fragrances & Flavors

Every night COMPASS reads seventy-five vetted sources on fragrance and flavour. Trade journals, the rulebooks in full, scientific papers, what Indian buyers wrote in reviews, what creators said in their reels, India's trademark journal. People at AARAV ask it questions in their own words. Each claim in an answer carries the sentence it came from, and when the library cannot support an answer, it says so.

VAAGDHARA Leadership Journey

The software that runs VAAGDHARA's journey for its next generation of leaders

The journey lasts twelve to eighteen months. A fellow is assessed, chooses a mentor, agrees a path, and writes one page by hand every week. The software holds all of it for fellows, mentors and the leadership group, in English and Hindi. The AI reads alongside the mentor. It never writes for the fellow, and nothing it says reaches a fellow before a named person has seen it.
