What to include

Tell me what the problem is, and what it is costing you, whether that cost is in time, money, missed opportunity, or organisational stress. Tell me what you have tried, and what you are hoping to change.

You do not need to pitch me, and you do not need to explain AI or economics. Just describe the situation as clearly as you can. The rest of the conversation will figure itself out.
